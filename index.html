What is FocusForge?

FocusForge is a simple productivity app that brings your daily tasks, habits, and focused work sessions into one place. Instead of juggling several apps, you can plan your day, build consistent habits, and track your progress from a single dashboard.

Features

Task Management

Add, complete, and filter your daily tasks by all, completed, or pending.

Habit Tracking

Mark habits done each day and build up a streak that keeps you consistent.

Pomodoro Timer

Work in focused 25-minute sessions with start, pause, and reset controls.

Progress Tracking

See completed vs. pending tasks, total focus time, and habit streaks at a glance.

Dark Mode

Switch to a dark theme that stays in sync everywhere you use FocusForge.

Simple Dashboard

One dashboard shows your tasks, habits, and focus time as soon as you log in.
